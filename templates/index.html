EV Battery Health & Diagnostic Cockpit

Understand battery condition, remaining lifetime, and how operating conditions (Cycles, Temperature, Driving Style) directly impact degradation.

Scenarios:
Cycle Count → Remaining Life (RUL)

Every charge/discharge cycle consumes active lithium ions through SEI layer growth. Higher cycles directly reduce remaining life.

Temperature → Aging Speed

Operating above 35°C accelerates chemical degradation 2× faster. Temperatures below 10°C reduce temporary efficiency and power output.

Resistance → Voltage Sag & Heat

As cells age, internal resistance ($m\Omega$) climbs. Higher resistance causes voltage drop under acceleration and generates internal heat ($I^2R$).

Capacity → Health (SOH)

Measured Capacity ($Ah$) reflects maximum stored energy. A fresh cell starts at 2.0 Ah (100% SOH) and reaches End-of-Life at 1.4 Ah (70% SOH).
